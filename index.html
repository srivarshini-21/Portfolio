01THE SHORT VERSION

A little about
how I work.

I’m drawn to the messy middle: unfamiliar problems, complicated data, and the moment a rough idea starts to feel like a real product.

At Latlong, I joined the work around Aaloka when it was still at the technical-feasibility stage. I helped take it toward a working geospatial SaaS product, moving between interface, backend, database and visualization work as the problem demanded.

That same end-to-end instinct shows up in my other projects too — from AI-assisted legal workflows to healthcare software. I care about how a system is built, but just as much about whether it makes sense to the person using it.
